Texas is large enough that an in-state vehicle move can cover hundreds of miles. Add the steady flow of relocations to and from Dallas–Fort Worth, Houston, Austin and San Antonio, and it becomes one of the country's most active auto transport markets.

## What determines a Texas car shipping quote?

Carriers consider the total mileage, vehicle dimensions, whether the vehicle runs, trailer preference, pickup and delivery access, and current demand. Fuel prices and the number of available trucks on a route also influence the final rate.

Open carriers provide excellent value for everyday vehicles. Enclosed carriers are a strong choice when added weather and road-debris protection matters more than the lowest possible price.

## Common routes to and from Texas

- Texas to California
- Texas to Florida
- Texas to New York and New Jersey
- Texas to Arizona
- Texas to Colorado
- Moves between Dallas, Houston, Austin and San Antonio

Major metro areas are usually easier for carriers to access. Rural pickups are absolutely possible, but meeting near a highway or allowing a wider pickup window may improve availability.

## When should you book?

Start planning one to two weeks before your preferred pickup date. Extra lead time is helpful around holidays, severe-weather periods and the beginning or end of the month. Your pickup date is normally a window rather than a guaranteed appointment until a carrier is assigned.

## Before the carrier arrives

Remove loose items, disable alarms, check for leaks and take clear photos of every side of the vehicle. The carrier must be able to steer, brake and roll the vehicle unless inoperable service was arranged in advance.
